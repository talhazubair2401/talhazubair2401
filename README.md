## Featured projects

### ✈️ [Nexora SafarPro](https://github.com/talhazubair2401/nexora-safarpro) — travel agency management
Bookings, air tickets, visas, Umrah groups, tours, payments and a full double-entry ledger, running offline on the agency's PC.
Nine staff roles enforced on the server, verified backups, and reports built straight from the books.
